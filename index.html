个人简介 / PROFILE

以产品判断连接技术，
以系统能力推进落地。

我是葛韵斐，同济大学机械工程博士，现任中国电信人工智能研究院（TeleAI）具身智能高级研究员，曾任西门子中国研究院高级研究员。我的工作聚焦具身智能产品规划、系统研发与项目组织，连接客户场景、研究成果与工程实现。

我主导的产品研发覆盖全尺寸与小尺寸人形机器人、轮式双臂机器人、仿生形态机器人、具身数据采集设备及遥操作系统。从产品方向与总体方案，到机械、电气、嵌入式软件、算法和交互的协同集成，我负责组织团队将分散的技术能力构建为完整产品。

我重视产品的形态、体验与细节，也关注成本、安全、维护和迭代。通过场景分析、技术取舍与项目推进，让机器人具备可验证的应用能力，并持续探索可复制的行业解决方案。博士期间的机器人机构学、动力学与医疗机器人研究，为这项工作提供了系统设计基础。

PROFILE / PRODUCTS & SYSTEMS

Defining the product.
Engineering the whole system.

I am Yunfei Ge, a Senior Researcher in Embodied AI at TeleAI, China Telecom. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Tongji University and previously worked as a Senior Researcher at Siemens Research in China. My work connects customer needs, research capabilities and product engineering.

I lead product definition and development across full-size and compact humanoids, wheeled dual-arm robots, biomimetic robots, embodied data collection devices and teleoperation systems. I organize hardware and product teams and collaborate with algorithm researchers to integrate mechanics, electronics, embedded software, control and interaction into complete systems.

I care about product form, interaction and the details of everyday use, alongside cost, safety, maintainability and iteration. My focus is on making deliberate engineering trade-offs, validating systems in real applications and developing repeatable solutions. Earlier research in mechanisms, dynamics and medical robotics underpins this systems perspective.
