个人简介

你好！我是葛韵斐，工学博士，毕业于同济大学机械工程专业。现任中国电信人工智能研究院 (TeleAI) 具身智能高级研究员。

我拥有深厚的机器人机构学与动力学背景，博士期间致力于并联外固定机器人及其智能矫形系统的研究。目前，我专注于人形机器人与具身智能遥操作系统的研发，成功主导了 TeleBot 系列人形机器人及 TeleHumOS 全身遥操作系统的搭建。我的研究目标是打通从“机械设计”到“智能控制”的全链路，推动通用机器人在真实场景中的应用。

Biography

Hi! I am Yunfei Ge. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Tongji University. I am currently a Senior Researcher at TeleAI (China Telecom Artificial Intelligence Institute), focusing on Embodied AI.

With a solid background in robot mechanisms and dynamics, my Ph.D. research focused on parallel external fixation robots and intelligent orthopedic systems. Currently, I am dedicated to the R&D of Humanoid Robots and Embodied Teleoperation Systems. I have successfully led the development of the TeleBot series humanoids and the TeleHumOS whole-body teleoperation system. My goal is to bridge the gap between mechanical design and intelligent control to promote the deployment of general-purpose robots in real-world scenarios.
